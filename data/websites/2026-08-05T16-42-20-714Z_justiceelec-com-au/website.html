Our Services

At Justice Electrical and Air Conditioning, we’re a small family-run electrical business with a big focus on quality, reliability, and service. Specialising in domestic maintenance and small commercial electrical work, we’re proud to deliver honest, dependable solutions for homes and businesses alike.

We believe in doing the job once—and doing it right. From fixing a faulty switch to handling complex wiring, every task is completed with care, precision, and a strong commitment to safety. No shortcuts, no surprises—just solid work you can count on.

With over 15 years of hands-on experience and a genuine passion for helping people, we bring friendly service, clear communication, and professional results to every job. Whether it’s your home or your workplace, we treat your space with the same respect and attention we’d expect in our own.

We’re not the biggest team, but we take pride in being the right one for the job.
