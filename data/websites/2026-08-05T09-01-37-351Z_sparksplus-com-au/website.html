About Sparks Plus

Your Local Electrician
in Sydney

WE ARE A LOCAL SYDNEY ELECTRICAL COMPANY PROVIDING RESIDENTIAL, COMMERCIAL & INDUSTRIAL ELECTRICAL SERVICES.

SPARKS PLUS ELECTRICAL is a trusted provider of fast and efficient electrical services in Sydney. Our electrical team is Fully Insured and Licensed, ensuring the highest level of professionalism and expertise.

We offer competitive rates on residential electrical services and commercial electrical services to all suburbs within the greater Sydney metro area. When you hire our electrical services, you can count on us for the results you want and need, with a qualified and reputable electrician in Sydney.
Please note that while we do provide quotations for our services, there is a call-out fee associated with the quotation process. However, this fee is fully redeemable on the work if you decide to proceed with our services. We believe in transparency and ensuring that our customers have all the necessary information before making a decision.

We pride ourselves on maintaining a high level of service and are dedicated to exceeding our clients’ expectations. Our satisfied customers continue to rely on us for their electrical needs, and we would be delighted to assist you as well.
