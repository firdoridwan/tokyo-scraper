Reliable Climate Control Experts in Brisbane

MegaChill Air Conditioning & Refrigeration is Brisbane’s trusted partner for all things cooling and climate control. Founded on the principles of reliability and quality service, we’ve earned a reputation for delivering effective solutions for homes, businesses, and industrial facilities throughout the Brisbane region. Our experienced team specialises in air conditioning and commercial refrigeration, providing comprehensive installations, repairs, and ongoing maintenance.

Whether it’s keeping your family comfortable or ensuring your business’s operations run smoothly year-round, we offer a broad range of services from split system air conditioners and ducted systems to industrial-grade refrigeration and walk-in freezer repairs. Every project, big or small, receives our full attention, honest advice, and a commitment to workmanship that stands the test of time.

Our Mission

We aim to be Brisbane’s top choice for dependable, energy-efficient, and cost-effective air conditioning and refrigeration. We support our clients by delivering fast response times, clear communication, and innovative solutions tailored to their specific needs.
