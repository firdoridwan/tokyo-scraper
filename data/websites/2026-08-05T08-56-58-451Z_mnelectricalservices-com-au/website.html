About Us

M&N Electrical Group is a locally and family-owned business offering residential, commercial, and industrial electrical services. We have been in the industry for over 15 years, meaning you can trust us to handle your electrical issues effectively. Our team works collaboratively to ensure that we deliver quality work. We understand that electrical problems do not only occur during office hours. For this reason, we offer 24/7 emergency services to ensure you are never inconvenienced.

Qualified Level 2 Electricians

A Level 2 electrician is authorised to connect residential or commercial premises to and from the electricity grid. Not all electricians can perform this type of electrical work since it is risky. A Level 2 electrician can connect and disconnect your power supply if you have a renovation project. At M&N Electrical Group, we are qualified Level 2 electricians, and we pride ourselves on delivering high-quality workmanship.
