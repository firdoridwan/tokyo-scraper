Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

We provide a full range of electrical services, including electrical installations, routine maintenance, safety inspections, fault finding, and emergency repairs. We specialise in ensuring that all electrical systems in common areas, including lighting, switchboards, and security systems, are functioning safely and efficiently.

Can you assist with electrical compliance and safety inspections for strata properties?

Yes, we can assist with electrical compliance and safety inspections for strata properties. Our licensed electricians conduct thorough inspections to ensure that your building complies with local electrical codes and safety standards. This includes checking switchboards, wiring, lighting, and smoke alarms, helping you avoid potential hazards and costly repairs.

What should I do if I have an electrical emergency in my home or business?

If you experience an electrical emergency, such as a power outage, sparks, or electrical smells, it’s important to turn off the power at the switchboard immediately, if safe to do so. Then, contact our emergency service team, & we’ll dispatch a licensed electrician to assess and resolve the issue right away.

How do you ensure minimal disruption to residents and tenants during electrical work in a strata property?

We understand the importance of minimising disruption in multi-tenant properties. Our team coordinates with property managers to schedule work during off-peak hours or during planned maintenance periods. We ensure clear communication with residents and take all necessary precautions to complete the job efficiently and with minimal noise and inconvenience.

Do you offer regular maintenance for electrical systems in homes and businesses?

Yes, we offer regular electrical maintenance services for both residential and commercial properties. Our maintenance packages include routine inspections, testing of safety devices (such as smoke alarms and RCDs), and checks on wiring and electrical equipment. Regular maintenance helps to prevent potential issues and extends the life of your electrical system.
