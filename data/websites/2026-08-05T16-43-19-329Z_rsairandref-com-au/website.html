About Us

Over 30 years of experience

RS Airconditioning and Refrigeration Pty Ltd is a leading air conditioning and refrigeration service provider based in South Australia. Established in 2012, with over 22 years of industry experience, we have built a reputation for delivering high-quality installations, maintenance, and repair services to both residential and commercial clients across Australia.

Originally founded as Raj and Sons Airconditioning, our company rebranded to RS Airconditioning and Refrigeration Pty Ltd to better align with our vision of providing exceptional customer satisfaction, both before and after-sales. Our growth has been fueled by a commitment to innovation, reliability, and excellence in service delivery.
