How often should I have a pest control treatment?
For most residential properties, a general pest control treatment every 6 to 12 months is a practical approach to managing common household pests such as cockroaches, ants and spiders. The appropriate frequency will depend on your property type, pest pressure and environment. We can provide a recommendation based on your specific situation.
What is included in a termite inspection?
A termite inspection is a visual, non-invasive inspection of accessible areas of the property. This includes subfloor areas (where accessible), roof void (where accessible), internal and external areas, weep holes, slab edges, timber in ground contact, garden beds against walls and moisture-affected areas. We assess for conducive conditions and provide practical recommendations in a clear written report. Inspections are conducted where visible and accessible — concealed or inaccessible areas cannot be assessed without further investigation.
How does body corporate pest control work?
We work with body corporate managers, committees and facilities managers to coordinate pest control across common property areas. This includes scheduling access, communicating with residents, treating common areas and providing clear photo-supported reporting suitable for committee records and maintenance registers. We understand body corporate workflows and the need for professional, compliant service.
What does rodent management involve?
Rodent management typically involves the installation of tamper-resistant bait stations in appropriate locations, monitoring of activity, bait replenishment and activity checks. We focus on safe placement away from children and non-target animals, and recommend ongoing monitoring programs where rodent pressure is ongoing.
What areas do you service?
We service Brisbane Northside and the Moreton Bay region, including Bracken Ridge, North Lakes, Redcliffe, Sandgate, Chermside, Aspley, Carseldine and surrounding suburbs. If you are unsure whether we cover your area, please call or send an enquiry and we will confirm.
What should I do to prepare for a pest control treatment?
We will advise you on preparation requirements when you book. Generally, this includes clearing under sinks and in cupboards, removing pets and their food bowls during treatment, and ensuring access to all required areas. We will provide clear instructions specific to the treatment being carried out.
Can you guarantee that termites will not return after treatment?
No responsible pest control operator can guarantee that termites will never return. Termite management systems and barriers significantly reduce the risk of termite activity and provide ongoing protection, but regular inspections and monitoring remain important. We provide practical recommendations and clear reporting to help you manage risk over time.
Are your treatments safe for children and pets?
We use registered pest control products applied in accordance with label directions and industry standards. We will advise you of any re-entry requirements and precautions specific to the treatment. If you have specific concerns regarding children, pets or health conditions, please let us know when booking.
