Our Services

Comprehensive Electrical Solutions for Every Property

At Greater Sydney Electrical, we provide a full range of residential, commercial, and strata electrical services designed to keep your home or business safe, efficient, and fully powered. With expert workmanship and reliable support, we deliver solutions you can trust—no matter the size of the job.

General Electrical

From small repairs to large installations, we provide fast, reliable, and compliant electrical solutions for homes and businesses.

Switchboard Upgrades

Old switchboards can be dangerous. We upgrade outdated panels to modern RCD-protected units to keep your property safe and compliant.

LED Lighting Solutions

Energy-efficient LED lighting that reduces your power bill while improving light quality.

Data, Phone & Communication

Professional installation of data points, NBN setup, networking cabling, intercoms, and alarms.

Wiring and Rewiring

We specialise in providing top of the range wiring solutions for a wide range of applications, including lights, switches, entire homes, and more

Electrical Maintenance

Regular electrical maintenance for homes, residential homes, commercial buildings, offices, strata complexes, and industrial sites.
